About Karabo Green Initiative

Karabo Green Initiative is a fictional community-based non-profit organisation established in 2022 in Limpopo, South Africa.

The organisation was created in response to concerns about illegal dumping, poor waste management and limited environmental awareness within local communities.

Our History

Karabo Green Initiative began as a community environmental awareness concept developed by local volunteers who believed that environmental education could encourage communities to take greater responsibility for their surroundings.

Since its establishment, the initiative has focused on community education, recycling awareness, clean-up activities and practical environmental information.

Our Mission

To educate and empower communities to adopt responsible environmental practices that contribute to cleaner and healthier communities.

Our Vision

To create environmentally aware communities where people actively participate in protecting and preserving their surroundings.

Our Values

  • Environmental Responsibility: We encourage responsible decisions that protect natural resources.
  • Community Participation: We believe lasting environmental change requires participation from communities.
  • Education: We provide accessible information that helps people make informed environmental decisions.
  • Sustainability: We encourage practices that support healthier communities and environments over the long term.
  • Accountability: We promote responsible action and transparency in our community activities.
